## Existing Page Purpose

Allow visitors to request a state or local workforce board impact report.

## Extracted Content

Start Your ROI Conversation. Get Your Board's Current Impact Report.

Impact reports are based on U.S. DOL Program Year 2025, Q1 data, covering Oct. 1, 2024 through Sept. 30, 2025.

The flow is: select a state, select a workforce board, enter contact information, and submit the request.
